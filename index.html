History

My Background

Growing up in the North East I was always glued to my parent's old consoles. I had been enthralled with the worlds of Hyrule and Peach's castle since a very young age.

I knew that I wanted to be a game developer, so I studied game development in college and game design at university, where I got a masters at a first degree.

During my time learning design, I discovered my affinity for programming. Unreal Engine's blueprints were a great introduction but I found myself enjoying them more than my peers at university.

Since then I've been developing my skills professionally in the workplace by moving onto C++ and when I'm home I'll work on personal projects in Godot.

Dink 2025 - Current

I currently work for Dink as their Lead Technical Designer. Producing stable code, perfromant builds, tracking issues, and writing documents to save our staff time.

Freelance 2024 - 2025

Worked with Dink and COPA Gaming to produce plugins such as the Unreal Accessibility toolkit, a replicated AI companion, and a quest system.

Omniplay Games 2023 - 2024

Starting as an intern, I worked with Omniplay to create internal projects and push updates for their existing game Farlife.
